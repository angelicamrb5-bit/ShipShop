Returns

Free returns within 7 days

Changed your mind or need another size? You can return items within 7 days of receiving them.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and with tags on.
  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number to start a return.
  • Bring it to our store, or we’ll arrange a pickup in Georgetown.
  • We exchange it or refund you the same way you paid.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Do I need an account to order?

No. Check out as a guest with your name, WhatsApp number and address. That’s it.

How do I know my order went through?

You’ll see your order number on screen, and we confirm on WhatsApp within 1 hour during store hours.

Can I order without using the website?

Yes. Send us a product link or a photo on WhatsApp and we’ll take your order in the chat. You can also visit our store.

What if I’m not sure about my size?

Check the size guide on the product page, or ask us on WhatsApp. Real people answer and will help you choose.

I live outside the listed zones. Can you deliver?

Choose “Other” at checkout or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm if we can deliver and the fee before you pay.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order number. If it hasn’t left the store yet, we’ll change it for you.
