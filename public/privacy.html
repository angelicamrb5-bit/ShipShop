Privacy

We only ask for what we need to deliver your order.

What we collect

When you order: your name, WhatsApp number, optional email, delivery area and address. We use them to confirm, deliver and support your order.

What stays on your device

Your bag, wishlist, checkout details you’ve typed and your recent orders are saved in your browser so nothing gets lost. Clear your browser data to remove them.

WhatsApp

When you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a message ready for you to send. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Questions

Chat with us on WhatsApp or visit our store.
